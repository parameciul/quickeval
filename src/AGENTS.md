# src/AGENTS.md

The two browser apps. The root `AGENTS.md` holds the project-wide rules.

## Structure

- `ui/`: brand CSS (the look of the redesign spec §2; the names are older than it: `--paper` is the ground, `--sheet` a surface, `--rule` a border, `--ink` the blue, `--marker` the tint of what waits on the teacher), the MQGrader mark (`BrandMark.tsx`: "Trei subiecte", three arcs coloured by `--mk-a` and `--mk-b`; `public/favicon.svg` draws the same arcs), the theme button and the phone menu's switch (`ThemeButton.tsx`; `useTheme.ts` keeps them in step), Romanian count labels and dates, the error boundary, `ApiError.ts` (the error type and JSON answer reader of both apps), `bucharestTime.ts` (the schedule input in Romania's time), `classTone.ts` (a class's colour: a hue stepped by the golden angle from the class id, so every class differs; the page sets `--tone-hue` and `--tone-light` with `classToneStyle()` and the class `class-tone`; used by the Clase cards and the test page's class link), `tabKeys.ts` (the arrows, Home and End of a tab list; the chosen tab alone has `tabIndex` 0; the class page and Setări use it). `MathText.tsx` draws the grading's Unicode math like on paper (`mathText.ts` finds √15, √(x + 1) and (x + 1)/3, and leaves intervals and words such as și/sau as text); every grading text a page shows (answers, comments, review reasons, summary, lists, the class analysis) goes through it, and the page text stays the same for copying and screen readers. A label (`.tag`, `.status`, `.class-badge`) is a flat tinted block, grey by default and orange (`.tag-wait`) only for what waits on the teacher; a button or link that acts is an 8px rounded outline (`.button-quiet`), filled blue (`.button`) for the one main action of a screen, and a card keeps its actions together in `.card-actions`.
- `admin/`: the teacher app. `api.ts` is the only code that calls the API; pages get it from `useApi()`, so tests pass a fake. `testPage/` holds the parts of the test page (redesign spec §6): the list of the six stages (`StageList.tsx`), the panel of the chosen stage (`StagePanel.tsx`: the controls of each stage, `TestActions`, and the papers table of În clasă, Corectare and Verificare, with its checkboxes only in Corectare), the panel Rezultate (redesign spec §8: "Publică toate rezultatele" and "Oprește accesul la rezultate" in `ResultsAccess.tsx`; once results are published (for all, or one alone), `StudentAccessCard.tsx`: where students find their results (the students' link with its QR code and "Descarcă codul QR", a PNG; the multi-upload link named "Doar pentru profesor", with no QR code; and the help text); the students' result links in `ResultLinksTable.tsx`, with the points, the grade, "Copiază linkul" ("Fă linkul" first for an older paper with no link) and the publishing of one result; "Pentru tine" in `ResultFiles.tsx`: the class report and its CSV; the result link and the publishing are only there, not in the papers table), Fișa testului (`TestSheet.tsx`: the facts, the files, the students' link while the test is a draft or open, rename, archive (it asks first in `ArchiveDialog.tsx` when results are open: `hasOpenResults`), delete), the points to check in Verificare (`ReviewList.tsx`: a row per point with its reason, each opening the review), the files of Pregătire (`TestFiles.tsx`), the evaluation controls, the barem warning in Corectare (`ExerciseListBanner.tsx`, with a link to Pregătire), a link in a panel to another stage (`MoveOnLink.tsx`: "Următoarea etapă", "Înlocuiește baremul"; the new panel's heading takes the focus), the robot line and its "Pornește robotul" button (`StartRobotButton.tsx`, in the panel of the robot's work: Pregătire for a barem, Tipărire for the print PDF, Corectare for the grading), the generated barem's button, waiting line and "Am verificat baremul" (`GeneratedBarem.tsx`, on the barem row; the page also refreshes every 10 s while a request waits), the uploads table (`UploadsTable.tsx`: "Vezi lucrarea" and "Reîncearcă" on the row, "Recorectează" and "Resetează" under the row's ⋯, a menu fixed to the screen so the table's sideways scroll does not cut it off), "Recorectează tot"). `pages/ReviewPage.tsx` and `reviewPage/` are the review of the points to check (redesign spec §7, `/teste/:code/verificare`): the papers and their points (`ReviewQueue.tsx`), one page of the paper at a time (`ReviewPhoto.tsx`: a photo zooms inside the pane (a left click, a right click and the two buttons over the photo take one small step, out and in; two fingers pinch on a touch screen, and the wheel scrolls the page), a line over the page says what a click does until she zooms, in the words of the pointer at hand (a phone has no right button), and the point picked draws an orange border around the robot's area of its answer, on the whole page, because the page never zooms by itself; the border's arrows step through the parts of an answer, each part opening on its page, whole), and the decision (`ReviewDecision.tsx`: "Accept", the stepper and "Salvează", "Am verificat paginile", the paper's exercises). `points.ts` orders the points (`reviewPoints` of the uploads list, as many as `flagCount`; the pages first), steps between them, and writes the address (`?lucrare=&punct=`, replaced, never a new step in the history). The title row's "Punctul anterior" and "Următorul punct" step the queue both ways, each while a point waits in that direction. A decision names the grading it was made on, takes the point out of the cached test at once, and opens the next point; a refused one reads the paper and the test again. An address that names no paper of the test is rewritten first (`ReviewLoader` draws `<Navigate replace>` to `firstSpot()`), before any paper is drawn: a refresh never moves the spot, and no decision is made on a spot the address does not hold. The next point opens only if the teacher is still at the decided one (a call-level `onSuccess` of `mutate`, which does not run once the page is gone), and its heading takes the focus (`focus({ preventScroll: true })`). An address of a paper with no result now (graded again) shows that paper with the reason (`submissionPage/waitingText.ts`, shared with the paper page); when the uploads list has another `gradedAt` than the paper read, the page reads the paper again. The keys A, J, K and 1–9 act unless the focus is in a field or in what opened from the bars (`.panel`, `.phone-menu`, `.user-menu`, or a button with `aria-expanded="true"`), never with Ctrl, Alt or Meta, and once for a key held down (`event.repeat`). `submissionPage/` holds the parts of a student's result page (the pages, the graded result with its corrections). The page (`pages/SubmissionPage.tsx`) has a head (`.submission-head`: the name, the test and the times on the left; "Toate notele elevului", the PDF buttons and "Alte acțiuni" on the right), then the strips of the result link and of the files, and the result. "Alte acțiuni" (`PaperActions`, `useSmallMenu`) is a menu of the rare actions that undo work, Recorectează (only for a graded result) and Resetează (red): each item shows what it deletes under its name (its accessible description), and choosing it still asks with `window.confirm`. On a phone the menu is as wide as the row of buttons. `GradedResult` draws everything inside `.graded-result`, so its look and its phone cards are the same on a Quick Try's page: the grade strip ("De verificat: N" orange, or "Nimic de verificat"), the exercises table in a card (below 48rem one card per exercise: the label, the points, the confidence and the pencil on the first line), and the three texts for the student as cards. An exercise to check shows its reason and the "Verificat" button together in a note (`.review-note`) in the comment cell; the actions cell holds only the pencil, or Salvează and Renunță while editing. `pages/StudentPage.tsx` is a student's history (redesign spec §11.2, `/elevi/:id`): "←" to the class the teacher came from (`StudentLinkState`, `{ fromClass }`, passed by the grades table's names and "Toate notele elevului, din toate clasele"; else the active class, else the newest), the name with "Redenumește" (the class page's `RenameDialog`), the line of the classes ("Clasa 10B în 2026-2027 · înainte în …"), and one card per school year and class with a row per started test: the points, the grade drawn by the grades table's `GradeCell`, the result drawn by the "Elevi" tab's `ResultCell`, "Lucrarea", and the grades table's legend. On a phone its tables scroll inside their cards. `pages/SettingsPage.tsx` is Setări (administrators only; `settingsPage/AccountsSection.tsx` approves and changes accounts). `wayIn/WayIn.tsx` is the way in (redesign spec §11.4): two halves under the top bar, the form on the left, and on the right the landing page's heading, line and six stages, word for word (`WayIn.test.tsx` reads `index.html` to check them); `State` draws a state's icon (mail, done, waiting), its heading (which takes the focus when the state changes) and its line. `pages/LoginPage.tsx` is the login and sign-up: `Layout.tsx` shows it in place of every page when `/me` answers 401 `login_required`, and the pages wait for `/me` before they ask the API. Its states: "Intră în cont", "Cont nou de profesor", "Am uitat parola", "Verifică-ți emailul" (after the sign-up or the link asked for), and "Contul tău așteaptă aprobarea" when the login answers 403 `account_pending` (no session: its "Ieși" only goes back to the login); every other refusal shows under the form. `pages/NewPasswordPage.tsx` (`/parola-noua#<token>`) sets the new password, with no login. `pages/ConfirmEmailPage.tsx` (`/confirma-email#<token>`) confirms a new account's email, without a login, and only on its button (mail programs open links to check them). Both use `WayIn`. `pages/AccountPage.tsx` is Contul meu (redesign spec §11.5): "Contul tău" (the tag, the counts, or a demo account's meters), "Parola" and "Ieși din cont"; no pay button while nothing is on sale. `account/DemoMeters.tsx` holds what a demo account has used (`demoUsage`: the one test is used once `testsEvaluated` is 1 or more, whatever the regrades; the papers on their way to the robot count, never over `DEMO_PAPER_LIMIT`), its line and its two meters, for Contul meu and the demo button. `pages/NewTestPage.tsx` is "Test nou" (redesign spec §11.7, `/teste/nou`; `?clasa=<id>` chooses the class): three numbered steps (the class and the title, the test, the barem) and beside them the card "După ce creezi testul". "Nu e în listă? Clasă nouă" (only without `?clasa`) opens `NewClassDialog` on the page; its `onCreated` chooses the new class instead of opening the class page. The files come through `newTest/FileDrop.tsx` (a click, or a file dragged onto it; checked as `TestFileInput` does). The barem is "Încarc baremul acum" (its file is sent only with this choice, and kept while another is chosen), "Generează baremul din test" (off until a test file is chosen; a refused test file moves the choice to "Mai târziu") or "Mai târziu". "Creează testul" makes the test, sends the test file, then the barem file, or asks for the generated barem only once the test file was sent; what fails after the test exists reaches the test page as a notice. A class with an active CES student shows them and the CES switch (on) after the first step; on, "Testul CES" and "Baremul CES" sit beside the class ones (`.step-pair`, stacked below 768px), sent after the class files, and the CES barem asked for after the class one; `createTest` gets `cesOff` only for such a class. Under the button, `MissingList` says what the test would still miss. `pages/ReportPage.tsx` and `reportPage/` are the class report (redesign spec §11.3; `shared/stats.ts` makes its numbers): one page, with the list of its parts (marked as the teacher scrolls: `currentPart` measures the parts on every scroll and resize; "Pe scurt" stays marked while nothing is laid out) and "Notele pe grupe" on the left, sticky under the top bar; on the right "Pe scurt" (the tiles), "Rezultatele elevilor" (the points of each exercise, then "Total" and "Nota"; an active student with no graded paper is a row that says why), "Pe exerciții" (with a bar for "% din maxim") and "Analiza clasei" (`AnalysisSection.tsx`). Below 56.25rem: one column, without the list of parts. `pdf/` makes the PDFs and shares them: the definitions are pure functions (`studentPdf.ts`, `classPdf.ts`; a student's PDF is made only on the paper's page, one student at a time), `renderPdf.ts` loads pdfmake only when a PDF is asked for, `share.ts` shares or saves, and `PdfActions.tsx` holds the buttons.
- `admin/shell/`: the teacher app's shell (redesign spec §4), drawn by `Layout.tsx`. The 80px bar (`Rail.tsx`, the menu "Meniu": Test nou, Teste, Clase, De făcut with its number, and Setări only for an administrator); the top bar (the lockup "MQGrader by Laura Miron", a demo account's button (`DemoButton.tsx`, redesign spec §11.6: a ring of the papers used and "Demo · 15 lucrări rămase", orange when none are left; it opens "Cont demo" with the meters of Contul meu), the school year (`YearSelect.tsx`), the theme, and the initials (`UserMenu.tsx`: "Contul meu", "Ieși din cont" through `useLogout.ts`); logged out, only the lockup and the theme); the small menus of the top bar share `useSmallMenu.ts` (closed by Esc with the focus back on their button, a click outside, a new page); one panel open at a time (`Panel.tsx`: "Clasele tale" in `ClassesPanel.tsx`, "Ce ai de făcut" in `TodoPanel.tsx`, and on a phone the menu under ☰ in `PhoneMenu.tsx`), closed by Esc, ×, or a new page, and still closed after Back to the page it was opened on; `useFocusBack.ts` gives the focus back to the button that opened it. A panel comes right after the bar in the page; on a phone the page under it is `inert` (`ui/useMediaQuery.ts`). A layer above a panel (a small menu of the top bar, the QR code on the whole screen) takes Esc first, in the capture phase, and marks it with `preventDefault()`, so the panel stays open. The shell reads the tests of the year after every change: a page that reads them again itself does not wait for that read (`void`, not `await`), so a save is not slowed down; only a row of the Teste page waits, because its buttons come from the list. For an administrator, "De făcut" also holds an urgent first card (red, "Urgent") when accounts that confirmed their email wait for approval (`Layout.tsx` reads `['accounts']`, the list Setări uses); it counts as one thing in the bar, whose number turns red, and its button opens Setări. `todo.ts` decides "De făcut" from the tests list: the four cases of spec §5 (the fourth: what a draft or open test misses, `missingOf` in `admin/missing.ts`), and before them the one case of an archived test: its results open for all or for some students (`archivedPublished`), "Rezultate publicate pe un test arhivat" at Rezultate, whose next step is the link "Oprește accesul" (the archive closes the results, so publishing there is an exception; nothing else of an archived test waits); `todoPath()` sends the points to check to the review, the rest to the test page. Below 900px the bar moves to the bottom of the screen and Setări, the school year, the theme, a demo account's papers and the account move under ☰ (CSS and `PhoneMenu.tsx`).
- `pages/TestsPage.tsx` is the start page, the Teste page of the redesign (spec §5): the greeting and today's date in Romania's time (`testsPage/greeting.ts`), "N lucruri te așteaptă ˅", which opens the list of what waits in place (`testsPage/TodoRows.tsx`: "De făcut" of every class of the year, as the bar counts it, whatever chip is chosen), the class chips and the box "Arată și testele arhivate", and the groups "În lucru" and "Terminate" (`testsPage/TestGroup.tsx`; `inWork()` in `shell/todo.ts` is the rule of the panel "Clasele tale" too). A row shows the test, the stage bar and the current stage, the papers, and one next step (`testsPage/nextStep.ts`): a link to the test (to the review for "Verifică 3 puncte"), or the button that publishes the results on the row; "Oprește accesul" stays on a row whose results are open (not on an archived test: its next step is already the link "Oprește accesul"). Below 768px each row is a block.
- `stages.ts`: the six stages of a test (Pregătire, Tipărire, În clasă, Corectare, Verificare, Rezultate), each done, waiting on the teacher, in progress or later, and the current one, from the tests list (`TestSummary.prepared`, `barem`, the counts and `averageGrade`). A stage waits on the teacher (orange) exactly when `todoOf()` has the test, at that stage. An archived test's closed results are its normal end: Rezultate is done, "închise · test arhivat". The test page uses it too: it opens on the current stage (from data read for this visit, not data cached from an earlier one), and `stageHash()` and `stageFromHash()` give each stage's panel its address (`#pregatire` … `#rezultate`). After the first load only the teacher changes the panel: a refresh or a button that moves the test to another stage keeps it. The Teste page's "Pornește corectarea" opens `#corectare`.
- `classPage/GradesTable.tsx` is the class page's grade table, in a card (students by tests, the oldest test on the left, a 156px column per test headed by its name and "date · state", orange when the test waits; a cell shows the grade, orange with a dot when points wait, the upload's state, or "—" when no paper was sent; the last row is "Media testului", no per-student average; draft and open tests keep a column; archived tests show only when asked). `classPage/ClassTests.tsx` is "Testele clasei", beside the table (under it below 1180px): the newest test first, each with its date, the papers sent, its stage (orange when it waits) and its one next step (from `testsPage/nextStep.ts`, the button that publishes the results included; filled blue when it waits), and the toggle "Arată și testele arhivate (n)" that reveals the archived tests in the table too. `classPage/ClassStudents.tsx` is the "Elevi" tab, one card split in two (stacked below 760px): the students on the left (search that ignores case and diacritics, orange dot when points wait, "+ Adaugă elevi"), the chosen student on the right (the first one at first; "Redenumește", "A plecat din clasă", "Șterge"; a table of the class's tests with the date, the points, the grade and "N puncte de verificat", the result ("Publicat", "Nepublicat" with "Copiază linkul", "După verificare", "Nu a trimis lucrarea."), and "Lucrarea"; link "Toate notele elevului, din toate clasele"). An archived class is the same page, read only (`readOnly` on `GradesTable`, `ClassTests` and `ClassStudents`): a strip "Clasă arhivată." with "Scoate din arhivă" above the head (one write with ⋯'s item), the tag "Arhivată" first in the facts, no "Test nou pentru această clasă", no "+ Adaugă elevi", no "Redenumește", "A plecat din clasă" or "Șterge" for a student, nothing orange, and "Raportul clasei" as each test's one step once a paper is graded; the one exception is an archived test with open results, which waits there as in "De făcut" (orange, "Oprește accesul", and the class's dot in "Schimbă clasa"). `classPage/ClassDialogs.tsx` holds the class page's dialogs: "Clasă nouă" (the name and the students, one per line, in one step; a class made before its students failed is not made twice), "Adaugă elevi" (early warnings for names already in the class or pasted twice), and the rename of a class or a student. They use `Modal.tsx`, the app's dialog: a title, ×, a dimmed backdrop, the focus on the first field and kept inside, Esc (in the capture phase, before a panel), and the focus back to the opener (or to `returnFocus` when the opener closed with its menu). `pages/ClassPage.tsx` is the class page (`/clase/:id`): the top line (the name, "Schimbă clasa" with the classes of the year, the current one marked, a dot for one that waits, the archived classes last in their muted group "Arhivate" (the only list that leads to them; an archived class's ⋯ has "Scoate din arhivă"), and "+ Clasă nouă"; the facts; "Test nou pentru această clasă"; ⋯ with "Redenumește clasa"/"Arhivează clasa", which asks first through `ArchiveDialog` when a test of the class has open results) and the two tabs ("Elevi" with its count). `pages/LastClassPage.tsx` is `/clase`: it opens the last class the teacher saw (kept in `localStorage` as `quickeval:lastClass`, else the first active one of the year), or, with no active class, offers "+ Clasă nouă" and links the archived classes. A link's state (`ClassLinkState`) goes on to that class page: `{ newClass: true }` (the panel's "+ Clasă nouă") opens that dialog on arrival, `{ archived: true }` (the panel's "Arhivate (n)") opens "Schimbă clasa" at "Arhivate". The old "page with all classes" (`ClassesPage`) and `ClassStudentsPage` no longer exist.
- The CES variant: `CesTag.tsx` is the mark next to a CES student's name in every teacher table that lists students (the class page's two tabs, the papers table, the links table, the class report, the review queue, the search, the student page's heading); Fișa testului lists the CES files; "De făcut" (`shell/todo.ts`) also asks to check a generated CES barem, or to generate it again (`TestSummary.cesBarem`), after the class barem's case and until the test is done; `testPage/CesVariant.tsx` holds "Varianta CES" in Pregătire (shown while the class has a CES student or the variant has a file: the CES students and the switch "Test separat pentru elevii cu CES" (`CesSwitch.tsx`, also on Test nou; off asks first when there are CES files, then removes them through `setCesOff`), its own `TestFiles` with `variant="ces"` while the switch is on, the total warning, and "Șterge varianta CES" only once the class has no CES student left) and the notice of the CES papers that wait for their barem in Corectare (`CesWaiting`); `testPage/variant.ts` picks a variant's parts of a test. What a draft or open test misses (`missing.ts`) is said in the stages, in "De făcut", as orange "lipsește" (and green "gata") marks in `TestFiles`, and as `MissingList.tsx` under "Începe testul" (with why each part matters) and under "Creează testul". `TestFiles`, `useGeneratedBarem`, `PrintTest` and `ExerciseListBanner` take `variant`. The class report, its CSV and the class PDF draw the tables of `reportTables` (`shared/stats.ts`): one, or the class papers then the CES papers with their own exercises; the CSV never names CES.
- `testPage/PrintTest.tsx` shows the test with the QR code ("Testul pentru tipărit") on a draft and an open test, its failure and retry, and "Pregătește testul cu cod QR" for a draft made before tests got their link at once. `TestActions` in `testPage/StagePanel.tsx` (the panel În clasă) holds "Începe testul" and "Oprește testul" (only while no upload has a file and the test was never evaluated). The student app also shows that screen when a name pick answers `not_started` (the test was stopped meanwhile). The student app shows "Testul nu a început încă." for a draft's link (the printed QR code is scanned before the start).
- `pages/SettingsPage.tsx` is Setări (redesign spec §11.8, administrators only): three tabs, with the tab in the address (`?tab=corectarea`, `?tab=robotul`; none, or one it does not know, is "Conturi", and a click replaces the address). The three panels stay drawn, the others `hidden`, so a new key or password shown once survives a look at another tab. "Conturi" (`settingsPage/AccountsSection.tsx`) holds a red-edged card of the accounts that wait (gone when none waits; "Refuză toate" from two up) and "Toate conturile", with ⋯ (`shell/useSmallMenu.ts`) on a teacher's row only: "Fă cont complet" or "Fă cont demo", "Parolă nouă", "Închide contul", and for a closed account "Redeschide contul". An item closes the menu and gives the focus back to ⋯ before it asks or acts; the new password and an error show in a row under the account. Every row, an administrator's included, has "Detalii", which opens "Activitatea contului" (what the account used, the grading of the last 30 days, the room its papers take, the classes, students and tests it made, the last login), "Consumul robotului" (by kind: what worked, what it gave up on, the tokens, and the failure rate), "Fiecare test" (each test's robot work, graded papers, corrected points and tokens) and "Istoricul contului" (its changes with their times, "de <cine>" when an administrator made one), from `GET /api/admin/accounts/:id`, read only when opened. "Corectarea" saves each control as it changes: the new value shows at once, the control stays on while it saves, and a refusal puts back the saved value. "Robotul" holds "Starea robotului" (a tag: "lucrează acum" or "nu lucrează acum") and "Cheia robotului" with the day it was made (`robotKeyAt`; a key made before that was kept has none). Below 40rem each account row is a block (CSS).
- `admin/search/` is search (redesign spec §11.10). `SearchBox.tsx` is the box after the lockup in the top bar ("Caută elevi, clase și teste", an ARIA combobox): from two letters, the list under it shows at most five of each group, "Elevi" (the classes on the right), "Clase" (the students, "arhivată"), "Teste" (the class and the date), with the match in bold (`Highlight.tsx`, `matchRange` of `shared/search.ts`), and "Toate rezultatele pentru „stef”" last. The arrows move (`aria-activedescendant`), Enter opens the chosen line or, with none chosen, the results page; a chosen line empties the box. Esc closes the list first and marks it with `preventDefault()`, so a panel stays open. `useSearch.ts` asks `/api/admin/search` 200 ms after the typing stops, keyed by the year and the folded text, and keeps the last answer on screen meanwhile; the shell reads `['search']` again after every change. `pages/SearchPage.tsx` is the results page at `/cautare?q=stef&arata=elevi`: "Rezultate pentru „stef”", how many in the school year, the chips "Toate", "Elevi", "Clase", "Teste" with their counts (an empty group's chip is disabled; an old link to it shows all), and one card per group: a student with the classes and "ultima notă" or "nicio notă încă", a class with its students, a test with its class, date and stage (`stagesOf`). On a phone the box gives way to a magnifier beside ☰ (`Layout.tsx`, the panel `search`): `SearchLayer.tsx` is the search over the whole screen, a dialog with ← "Închide căutarea" and the list always open; ←, Esc, a new page and a screen grown past a phone close it, the page, the top bar and the bar under it are `inert`, and the focus goes back to the magnifier. The class page's student search uses the same fold.
- `EmptyState.tsx` is an empty place of the teacher app (redesign spec §11.9): an icon (`shell/icons.tsx`), a heading at the level of its place (`level`: an h3 under a panel's or a card's title, an h2 in a card with none, an h1 on a page with no other heading), one sentence, and at most one button, its one next step. ## Gotchas

- A PDF never carries what is for the teacher only: `studentPdfInput()` is the only way into the student's PDF, and its type has no confidence and no review reason. Keep it so, and keep no word about how the work was graded in it. CSV cells that hold text go through `csvText()` (`shared/csv.ts`), which guards against formulas.
- The class PDF and the report page have one column for each exercise (up to 60): the page table scrolls in its `.table-wrap` and uses `report-table`, not the `uploads` table that turns into cards on a phone. The grade table of the class page does the same. The page table puts the exercises first, then Total and Nota (each exercise's maximum under its short name); the class PDF and the CSV keep their order.
- Dark colours in `ui/brand.css` are written twice (the `[data-theme="dark"]` block and the `prefers-color-scheme` block): keep both copies equal, the class colours included (`ui/brand.test.ts` checks it). The styles are flat: no gradients, and a shadow (`--float`) only on what floats (panels, menus).
- The top bar's account button is named "<initials>, contul tău: <name>" ("LM, contul tău: Laura Miron": the name starts with what the button shows), ☰ is named "Mai mult" (the bar is "Meniu"), and the phone menu repeats Setări, the school year, a demo account's meters, "Contul meu" and "Ieși din cont" while it is open: a page test that looks for one of these, or for a button by the teacher's name, looks inside `screen.getByRole('main')`.
- Testing Library's waits (`findBy…`, `waitFor`) last 3 s (`src/test/setup.ts`): while the whole suite runs, the first render of a page in a file can take about a second.
- The Teste filters live in the address: `?clasa=<id>` and `?arhivate=1`. A `clasa` that is not a class of the year counts as all classes.
- `TestSummary.averageGrade` is made by `averageGrade()` of `shared/stats.ts`, the function of the class report's statistics: keep both on it, so the Teste page and the report show the same number.
- In page tests, the fake tests list makes `prepared` from a test's `files`, `print` and `uploadToken`, and `averageGrade` from its graded uploads, as the server does: a test that must be at a later stage gives them (an open test without a test file is still at Pregătire). `fakeSummary()` builds one entry of the list for the pure functions; the parts of `prepared` an override leaves out keep their ready values.

- The Model column of the uploads table on the test page (`TestDetail.showModel`; the class report has none), and the model lines on Setări, are for the teacher only: the global setting `show_model_column` (the `settings` table, off by default) turns the column on. Student PDFs, the CSV, the class PDF and student pages never carry the model or effort.
- The schedule input takes Romania's time (`ui/bucharestTime.ts`).
- The Vite dev proxy keeps the Host header (`changeOrigin: false`). Otherwise the API refuses every write as cross-site.
- Page tests that check a navigation use `expectLocation()`: it waits, because the navigation follows the API answer. `LocationProbe` shows the path, the search and the hash.
- The review draws its right pane anew while another paper loads: after a move to another paper, its tests find the pane again through `screen`, not through an element found before the move. `reviewPointsOf()` in `test/fakeApi.ts` makes an upload row's `reviewPoints` from a fake evaluation, and the fake API keeps them in step after every correction.
- The test page draws only the panel of the chosen stage. Its page tests open a stage by its address (`/teste/6E2-26T1#corectare`) or by its link in the list of stages (`openStage()` in `TestPage.test.tsx`). Without an address the page opens on the current stage of `stagesOf()`: fake data with no test file opens on Pregătire, an open test with its files on În clasă, a done test with points to check on Verificare, a done test with none on Rezultate (which has the links table, not the papers table: a test of the papers table opens `#corectare`). Fișa testului is a region beside every panel: its students' link, its "Copiază linkul" and its buttons are found from any stage.
