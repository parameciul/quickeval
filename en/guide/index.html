How to use MQGrader, step by step

A test goes through six stages, from the files on your computer to the grade the student sees. Below you find each stage, in order: what you do, what the students do and what MQGrader does.

Written by Laura Miron, mathematics teacher · updated 21 November 2026

The six stages of a test, in order

Every test goes through the same six stages, whether 12 students take it or 34. The order matters, because each stage prepares what the next one needs. The pictures shown are screenshots of the real app.

1

Prepare

You

You need two files: the test and the marking scheme. Many teachers have the test in a document, and the marking scheme only in their head or on a handwritten sheet.

  • In MQGrader: you upload the test and the marking scheme. No marking scheme ready? You make one from the test automatically, then review and adjust it.
  • Handwritten? That works too: the test and the marking scheme can be photos of handwritten sheets, put in a PDF or a Word document.

2

Print

You

The test is printed before the lesson, with a QR code on every page. The student scans the code on any page and goes straight to where they send their paper.

  • In MQGrader: you download the PDF to print, with the QR code already on every page.
  • Printed the test without the code? If the papers were printed from the version of the test without the QR code, show the students the test's QR code or give them its link, from the app. That way they can upload their photos.

3

In class

Students

Students write the test as usual, then photograph its pages with their phone. They need no account and no app: the QR code on the test (or a link) and the phone's camera are enough. The photos reach you grouped by student, not as a heap of nameless files.

  • On the student's phone: they pick their name, take the photos, press “I sent everything” and get a ticket with a code, for the result.

4

Grading

You start it MQGrader

This is where the points are set. Every exercise is split into steps, and every step gets the points from the marking scheme. Reading the photos, finding each exercise, finding the step in the marking scheme, adding up the points — can be done without you. The decision cannot.

  • In MQGrader: you press “Close uploads and start grading”. Every paper is scored step by step, by the marking scheme.
  • On real papers: the exercises are found correctly even when the solutions have crossings-out, run over several pages or are not written in order.

5

Review

You

What is left are the debatable points: a solution shorter than the one in the marking scheme, an unusual notation, a dark photo, a step the student did in their head and did not write down. You check these: you accept the proposed points or change them. The decision stays yours.

  • Without the paper sheet: the answer is framed on the photo of the paper, next to the reason. It works from a phone too.

6

Results

You publish Students

At the end, each student sees their result: the grade, the points for each exercise, what they solved right and what not, what they did well and advice for what comes next. The teacher keeps the class report — which exercises went well and which did not — and the grades ready for the grade book.

  • For the student: the grade and the points for each exercise, with explanations, what they did well and advice.
  • For you: the class report, detailed point by point, with advice and statistics — in the app and as a PDF.
