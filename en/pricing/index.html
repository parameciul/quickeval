Why there is no price on this page

Because the price is not set yet. MQGrader is in development, and a subscription launched now would be a promise the product cannot keep yet. When there is a price, you will find it here and you will be told in your account too.

Nothing turns on by itself

You do not need a card to create your account, and you can try the platform right away with the demo account. There is no trial period that turns into a subscription by itself.

How to ask for an account

  1. You ask for the account.You press “Ask for a teacher account” and fill in your name, your email address and a password.
  2. You confirm the address.You get an email and confirm your address, so we know it is yours.
  3. The account is approved.An administrator approves your account. Until then, the account waits.
  4. You start.You sign in and start with a class and a test.

The approved account is a demo account, subject to the limits above. If you like it and need more, you can ask for a full account at contact@mqgrader.ro.
