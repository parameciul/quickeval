What the app looks like

No more late nights lost grading tests!

MQGrader.ro is a web platform for teachers that makes grading tests simple: students upload photos of their papers straight from their phones, and every paper is scored quickly, by the marking scheme.

Discover the new platform that takes a great weight off your shoulders and gives you rigorous grading, exactly by the marking scheme, in just a few minutes.

What makes it different? Control stays 100% with you. We know every student is unique and nuanced cases can come up, so the platform lets you change the points at any time in debatable or creative cases.

How does our platform help you?

  • Accuracy by the marking scheme

    The system scans and assesses the papers following closely the criteria you set, removing adding-up mistakes and the tiredness built up after hours of grading.

  • Full control in debatable cases

    We know students sometimes surprise with creative wording or other approaches. The platform gives you the freedom to adjust the points quickly at any moment, keeping the final decision strictly in your hands.

  • A huge saving of time

    You go from hours of grading to just a few minutes, time you can use for yourself or to give your students real feedback.

A test day, with MQGrader.

  1. Monday the day before

    You upload the test and the marking scheme.

    MQGrader prepares the test for printing, with a QR code on every page. No marking scheme ready? You make one from the test automatically, then review and adjust it.

    You MQGrader
  2. 11:45 the last minutes of the lesson

    Students photograph their paper.

    They scan the QR code on the test, pick their name and send the photos. No account and no app. Each gets a ticket with a code, for the result.

    Students have no phones? You take the photos of the papers later and upload them with your link for several papers.

    Students You
  3. 11:55 after the lesson

    You start the grading.

    One button: “Close uploads and start grading”. Every paper is scored step by step, by the marking scheme, with partial points. You get a notice to check only what is not sure.

    You MQGrader
  4. 15:30 from wherever you are

    You check the unsure points.

    From your laptop or your phone. The answer is framed on the photo of the paper, next to the reason. You accept or choose other points, without looking for the sheet.

    You
  5. 16:00 the same day

    You publish. Students see their result.

    MQGrader prepares the class report, and for each student a link and a PDF with their result. You press one button: “Publish the results”. Each student sees their grade, with explanations and points for every exercise.

    MQGrader You Students

What you no longer do.

No more accounts for students. No more emails with photos to collect. No more scanning papers. No more adding up points. No more feedback for each student to write.

The whole test day happens in one place: from the marking scheme to the grade in the grade book.

What you get on top.

  • Review from your phone tooThe unsure point is framed on the photo of the paper, next to the reason.
  • Explanations for every studentFor each exercise: what was right, what was wrong, and why.
  • The class reportThe average, the grades by band, what went well and what to go over again.
  • Tests for students with SENAn adapted version, with its own marking scheme, which only the student with SEN gets.
  • The history of every classAll the tests and grades of the class, in one place.
