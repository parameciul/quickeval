How to grade tests with MQGrader

Grading a test means whole hours of reading papers, looking up steps in the marking scheme and adding up points. MQGrader does the repetitive part: it reads the photos, finds every solution and proposes the points step by step, by your marking scheme. You check only what is unsure and decide the final grade.

Where the time goes

A test with nine exercises, given to a class of 30 students, means 270 solutions to score. If one solution takes you 50 seconds — you find it on the sheet, read it, find the step in the marking scheme, write the points — you reach 3 hours and 45 minutes for a single class, with no breaks. With three classes, a weekend is gone.

270solutions, for a class of 30
3 h 45 minfor one class, at 50 seconds a solution
11 h 15 minfor three classes: a weekend

The time is not lost on the clear cases: a right answer is seen at once, and so is a wrong one. It is lost on the debatable cases — exactly the ones you are tempted to rush, to finish.

How MQGrader grades a paper

From the photos a student sends to the proposed points, every paper goes through the same steps:

  1. It reads the photos. It understands handwriting in Romanian or English, with diacritics, symbols and notation, from photos taken with a phone, even with crossings-out or corrections on the sheet.

  2. It finds every exercise. Also when a solution runs on to another page, has crossings-out or the exercises are not solved in order.

  3. It scores step by step, by the marking scheme. Every step done gets its points, not only the final result. A mistake early in an exercise is not penalized again in the correct steps after it, in the same exercise. It works for open tasks too: short answers, solutions in steps, essays.

  4. It flags what is unsure. Hard-to-read writing, a cut photo, a method the marking scheme does not foresee: the point comes to you, with the reason.

  5. It writes explanations for the student. What they did well, where the mistake is and which step helps them fix it.

How you review the unsure points

You do not go through every paper again. You open only the flagged points, one after another, and you decide each one:

  • You see the answer framed on the photo of the paper, next to the reason it was flagged. No more looking for the paper sheet.
  • You accept the proposed points or type others, and see the resulting grade at once.
  • You move to the next point with a click or from the keyboard, from one student to the next too. It works from a phone as well.
  • You can change any exercise, not only the flagged ones: the points, the student's answer and the comment.

What can be automated and what cannot

Reading the photos, finding the solution of each exercise and proposing the points, step by step, can be automated. MQGrader uses artificial intelligence to read the paper and find, in each solution, the steps of the marking scheme — partial points included, not only the final result.

What cannot be fully automated is the decision in a case the marking scheme does not foresee. A student who solved it differently, but correctly, in part or in full, must get the appropriate points, and that is a decision for the teacher. The app helps you find the solution and check whether it is right, and proposes points, which must be confirmed by a teacher.

In the tests MQGrader has graded so far side by side with a teacher, on the same papers, the final grade differed by less than 0.3 points out of 10.

The rule MQGrader is built on

The sure points grade themselves. The unsure ones wait for you. The results reach the students only when you publish them.

The marking scheme, the rule behind the points

MQGrader grades by your marking scheme: every exercise is split into steps, and every step has its own points. That way the same solution gets the same points across the whole class, and every partial point can be explained to the student.

To grade the whole class the same way, every test needs a marking scheme. You have two ways:

1You upload your marking scheme.

It is a file with the points, as you already have it. The grading uses it exactly as it is.

2You make the marking scheme from the test.

You upload only the test, and MQGrader makes the marking scheme automatically: the steps of each exercise and their points. Then you review it, adjust it if needed and confirm it.

Frequently asked questions

  • Who sets the final points?

    The teacher. The proposed points follow the marking scheme, and the unsure points wait for your review: you accept or change them. The decision stays yours.

  • What happens if a student solves it differently from the marking scheme?

    The exercise comes to you as a point to check, with the reason. If the solution is right, you give it the full points, even if the method is not the one in the marking scheme.

  • What happens if a photo cannot be read?

    The page comes to you for review, with the exercises that could be on it. You look at the photo or at the student's sheet and decide the points yourself.

  • Which subjects can MQGrader grade?

    Mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, science, Romanian and English. The test can have multiple choice, short answers, solutions in steps or essays.

  • Can I upload a marking scheme I wrote?

    Yes. The marking scheme you upload is used exactly as it is. A generated marking scheme is only a draft, which you review, adjust or replace.

  • When should I publish the results?

    As soon as possible, before the next test. A student who waits a week for their grade learns nothing more from it, and the talk about the old test piles up on the new one.
